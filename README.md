# Scratch Solutions - Full-Stack Web Platform 🚀

Welcome to the official repository for the **Scratch Solutions** website. This project is a custom full-stack web application designed to serve as a high-converting marketing hub, dynamic portfolio, and automated contact portal for potential clients.

## 🎯 Primary Goals
* Market Scratch Solutions' services to potential clients.
* Provide a seamless contact, quoting, and invoicing experience.
* Serve as a dynamic CMS for updating projects and vlogs.
* Host a dedicated job application and careers portal.

## ✨ Core Features
* **Modular Architecture:** Clean, highly organized ("hiwahiwalay") layout for easy navigation.
* **Role-Based Access:** 
  * **Admin:** Full site control, content updates, and invoice management.
  * **Client:** Secure access to invoices and project communications.
* **Integrations:**
  * **Google Workspace:** Seamless syncing for emails and calendars.
  * **Google AI Chatbot:** Smart handling of customer inquiries and support.
* **Dynamic Portfolio:** A robust content management system to easily update Scratch’s ongoing projects and video logs.

## 🎨 Design System
* **Aesthetic:** Google-inspired UI (Clean, minimalist, card-based).
* **Color Palette:** White, Black, and Yellow.
* **UX Focus:** 100% responsive across all devices, human-crafted ("Not AI looks"), and highly organized.

## 🚀 Future Roadmap (Phase 2)
* **Staff Portal:** Dedicated login for employees.
* **Time Tracking:** "Time Doctor" style feature to monitor staff hours and activity.
* **AI Reporting:** Automated, AI-generated productivity and movement reports for internal team management.
